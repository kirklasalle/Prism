Business Trust Profile

Prism Refraction is designed for organizations that need autonomous execution with bounded authority, clear approvals, and auditable operational history.

Bounded Autonomy

Tiered policy enforcement constrains risky actions and routes elevated operations to explicit approval workflows.

Auditability

Session activity can be traced through signed event history, enabling post-action reviews and governance reporting.

Deployment Flexibility

Support local-first, staged enterprise rollout, and controlled profile enforcement for teams with different risk postures.

Control Matrix

Reference controls for security, platform, and operations evaluators.

Control Domain Current Implementation Surface Business Outcome
Policy Gates Tiered tool and mutation controls with approval barriers for higher-risk actions. Reduced operational blast radius and explicit governance checkpoints.
Change Traceability Session-level execution records, hash-linked integrity checks, and audit pathways. Faster incident review and stronger compliance readiness posture.
Profile Controls Individual vs Business execution boundaries and policy overlays. Safer delegation aligned to team maturity and risk tolerance.

Deployment Paths

  • Individual environment for rapid engineering loops.
  • Business profile with tightened execution boundaries.
  • Staged enterprise rollout with approvals and audit checkpoints.
