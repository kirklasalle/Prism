Privacy Policy

How Prism Refraction handles website interaction and contact form data.

Data Collected

  • Contact form data: name, email, subject, message.
  • Basic request metadata used for abuse prevention and service operations.

Use of Data

  • Respond to inquiries and coordinate demos or support discussions.
  • Protect service integrity via anti-spam and abuse controls.

Retention and Access

Contact submissions are retained only as long as necessary for operational and legal purposes.
